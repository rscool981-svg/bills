Help — how to use MEDBOOKS

Members (visible only to the app admin)
Keep every customer here. After the renewal date plus the grace days (7) the app logs that member out automatically on their devices, once you have published the list (see "Publish" below). Tick "Log out this member" to log someone out at any time.

Reminder message

Words in braces are replaced: {name} {date} {amount} {grace}. Used by the Gmail and WhatsApp reminder buttons.

Publish (makes the lock work on your members' devices)

Also lock companies that are NOT in this list (turn on only after every customer is added)
Upload the downloaded file members.json to your GitHub site, in the same place as index.html, replacing the old one. It holds only a scrambled form of each Gmail, the renewal date and the log-out tick; names, phone numbers and amounts never leave this tab. Devices read it when online, remember it, and also work from the remembered copy when offline. Do this again after every change you want your members to feel.

Language

Menus, tabs, buttons, labels and column headings change. Party names, stock item names and everything you type stay exactly as you typed them. This choice is kept on this device.
Google translates the whole screen, including names, so use it only for reading. Needs internet.

Shortcut keys

Tap Change on any row and press the new keys. Use Alt, Ctrl or Shift with a key, or a function key (F1 to F12). Backspace clears a shortcut, Esc cancels. Each device keeps its own list. On a phone, Run performs the action, or connect a keyboard.

Built-in keys (always on)

Enter: OK, choose the highlighted party or item, then jump to the next box (in the last row it adds a new row). Tab / Shift+Tab: next / previous box. Up / Down arrow: move between rows or choose from the party and item list. Left / Right arrow: move between boxes in a row. Esc: close the list, window or this settings screen.

Google Drive for this company

The Client ID comes from Google Cloud: Credentials, then OAuth 2.0 Client IDs, type Web application. If Google shows "Error 401: invalid_client", the ID here is wrong or was created in another project. Copy it again.
Everything for this company goes into one folder, "MEDBOOKS - company name", in that Gmail's Drive. Another phone that signs in with the same Gmail gets the same data.

Backup and restore

Restore adds what is missing and never overwrites. If the same bill was changed on two phones, the later change is kept.

Delete company

This removes the company and all its vouchers, ledgers and stock from this phone. A backup file is downloaded first. It cannot be undone.

Security

The password is asked for every modify, cancel, number change, restore, settings change, fiscal year closing and company delete. Passwords are stored only as a salted, slowed-down hash (PBKDF2). The app locks after 10 minutes without use. After 5 wrong passwords it waits 1 minute, and longer after repeated failures.
