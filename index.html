Shortcut keys

Tap Change on any row and press the new keys. Use Alt, Ctrl or Shift with a key, or a function key (F1 to F12). Backspace clears a shortcut, Esc cancels. Each device keeps its own list. On a phone, Run performs the action, or connect a keyboard.

Built-in keys (always on)

Enter: OK, choose the highlighted party or item, then jump to the next box (in the last row it adds a new row). Tab / Shift+Tab: next / previous box. Up / Down arrow: move between rows or choose from the party and item list. Left / Right arrow: move between boxes in a row. Esc: close the list, window or this settings screen.

Everything for this company goes into one folder, "MEDBOOKS - company name", in that Gmail's Drive. Another phone that signs in with the same Gmail gets the same data.

Backup and restore

Restore adds what is missing and never overwrites. If the same bill was changed on two phones, the later change is kept.

Delete company

This removes the company and all its vouchers, ledgers and stock from this phone. A backup file is downloaded first. It cannot be undone.

Security

The password is asked for every modify, cancel, number change, restore, settings change, fiscal year closing and company delete. Passwords are stored only as a salted, slowed-down hash (PBKDF2). The app locks after 10 minutes without use. After 5 wrong passwords it waits 1 minute, and longer after repeated failures.
